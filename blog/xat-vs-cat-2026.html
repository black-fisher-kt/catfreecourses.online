XAT vs CAT 2026: Decision Making, Pattern & Colleges Compared

Updated September 2026 · 6 min read

XAT and CAT are the two most respected MBA entrance exams in India, and many serious aspirants prepare for both together. But they test genuinely different skills — XAT's unique Decision Making section has no equivalent anywhere in CAT. Here's the full comparison.

The Decision Making Section

This is XAT's defining feature and has no parallel in CAT. It presents business scenarios, ethical dilemmas, and managerial judgment calls where you pick the "least bad" option among several plausible ones — there's often no clearly "correct" answer, which makes it feel very different from straightforward aptitude questions.

Decision Making typically carries around 21 questions and requires dedicated practice separate from your CAT prep — reading comprehension skills help, but the scenario-based judgment style needs its own practice sets.

Which Is Tougher?

Both exams are rated moderate-to-difficult, but they're hard in different ways:

  • CAT is harder in Quant and demands precision under strict sectional time pressure — you can't revisit a completed section.
  • XAT is harder in Verbal Ability and uniquely challenging in Decision Making, plus its ~3-hour length tests sustained concentration that CAT's 2-hour format doesn't.

Many aspirants find XAT's overall structure slightly tougher due to the added Decision Making and GK sections requiring entirely separate preparation on top of standard aptitude prep.

Colleges Accepted

  • CAT — All 21 IIMs plus 1,000+ other B-schools like FMS Delhi, MDI Gurgaon, SPJIMR, and IIFT.
  • XAT — XLRI Jamshedpur (the exam's own conducting body) plus 250+ colleges including XIMB, IMT, TAPMI, FORE, and Jaipuria.

If XLRI specifically is your goal, XAT is mandatory — XLRI doesn't accept CAT scores. For IIMs, only CAT works.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CAT preparation help with XAT?

Yes, largely. CAT prep covers most of the XAT syllabus (Quant, Verbal, Logical Reasoning), but Decision Making and General Knowledge need separate, dedicated preparation.

Is XAT harder than CAT?

Many aspirants consider XAT slightly tougher overall due to its additional Decision Making and GK sections, though CAT's stricter sectional time locks make it more demanding in a different way.

Can I take both CAT and XAT in the same year?

Yes, and many serious aspirants do — the exams fall in different months (CAT in November, XAT in January), and the syllabus overlap makes preparing for both manageable together.

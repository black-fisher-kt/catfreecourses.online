SNAP vs CAT 2026: Which MBA Entrance Exam Should You Take?

Updated September 2026 · 6 min read

SNAP and CAT are both MBA entrance exams, but they lead to almost entirely different college ecosystems and test very different things. If you're deciding whether to prepare for one, both, or are wondering how much overlap exists in preparation, here's the complete comparison.

Difficulty Level

CAT is widely considered more challenging than SNAP — it's a higher-stakes, single-attempt exam with strict sectional time locks that demand precision under pressure. SNAP, being speed-based with no sectional time limit, tests how quickly you can identify and solve manageable questions rather than deep conceptual difficulty. Its syllabus overlaps heavily with CAT, but the actual question difficulty is rated easy-to-moderate compared to CAT's higher bar.

That said, SNAP's 60-minutes-for-60-questions format leaves very little room for error in time management — knowing concepts isn't enough; quickly identifying which questions to attempt first matters just as much.

Colleges Accepted

  • CAT — Accepted by over 1,000 MBA colleges across India, including all 21 IIMs, FMS Delhi, MDI Gurgaon, IIFT, and SPJIMR.
  • SNAP — Accepted only by the 17 Symbiosis constituent institutes (like SIBM Pune and SCMHRD) and a handful of affiliated colleges such as JK Business School.

This is the biggest practical difference: SNAP does not open doors to IIMs. If an IIM seat is your goal, CAT is non-negotiable.

Which One Should You Take?

  • Take CAT if your target is an IIM or any of the 1,000+ B-schools in its wider network, and you're comfortable with high-pressure, single-attempt, sectional-time-locked exams.
  • Take SNAP if Symbiosis institutes specifically interest you, or you want a lower-pressure, multiple-attempt backup exam alongside your CAT preparation.
  • Take both if possible — the syllabus overlap means SNAP prep barely adds extra load once you're deep into CAT preparation, and it widens your admission options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SNAP easier than CAT?

Generally yes. SNAP is rated easy-to-moderate in difficulty compared to CAT's higher bar, though its 60-minute time-pressured format still demands strong speed and accuracy.

Can SNAP score get me into an IIM?

No. SNAP scores are accepted only by Symbiosis-affiliated institutes and a few partner colleges — IIMs require a valid CAT score.

Is the SNAP syllabus the same as CAT?

Broadly yes — both cover Quant, Verbal, and Logical Reasoning — but SNAP has no sectional time limit and is entirely MCQ-based, unlike CAT's MCQ + TITA format.
